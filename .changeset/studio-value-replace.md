Search and replace text across your translations from the Translations page.

- **Two steps:** **Search and replace** lists up to 1,000 matching values with an excerpt before and after, and writes nothing until you confirm.
- **What is left alone:** values a person wrote, imported or changed outside verbatra, and `pinnedKeys`, stay out unless you tick **Also change** in that run. Replaced values are recorded as written by a person (`human`).
