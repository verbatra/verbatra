Preview an ICU message in the translation editor.

- **Where:** a collapsed **ICU preview** under the draft in the Translations editor and the Review edit dialog, for `next-intl-json` and `arb` projects.
- **What you see:** one row per arm of your draft, with sample values you can type for any argument. Numbers and dates use this computer's language data, so the text can differ from your application's runtime.
